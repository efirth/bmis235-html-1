Cats have emotions too

Cats can feel a wide range of emotions, including happiness, fear, anger, and even disgust! They can also feel affection and attachment towards people and animals.

Cats don't show emotion like humans do.

Something to consider in cats emotions

  • Cats wagging their tails can mean they're either happy and calm or irritated. Look for other signs of emotion if a cat is wagging its tail.
  • Cats purring can mean they're stressed or they're relaxed. If you can't tell what emotion they're feeling, look to see if their eyes are dilated or not
  • When a cat's pupals are small and in a sharp slit shape, they are in hunting/high alertness mode.
